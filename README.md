# Impact of Autumn North Atlantic SST on European winter Temperature 
This repository contains the code and experiments conducted for my thesis work.

### Abstract
Autumn North Atlantic sea surface temperature (SST) have been identified as significant precursors to winter North Atlantic Oscillation and European winter temperature varia- tions. Despite its significance, it has not been possible to generalize the identification of autumn North Atlantic SST patterns and key regions that align with the classification of warm and cold European winters and impact of autumn North Atlantic SST anomalies on winter 2-meter temperature (2mT) patterns and key regions of Europe a↵ected by these anomalies. This study utilizes 50 historical simulations (1850–2014) from the CMIP6 Max Planck Institute Grand Ensemble dataset to explore these links, employing explain- able artificial neural network method. We find that tripolar North Atlantic SST pattern with warmer Subpolar Gyre (SPG) SST anomalies are critical for cold winter while warm winters are associated with the opposite tripolar pattern with warmer Subtropical Gyre (STG) SST anomalies. Furthermore, spatial patterns of winter 2mT anomalies di↵er between warm and cold autumn SST. During cold autumn SST, Northern Europe expe- riences warmer winters whereas Southern Europe experiences colder winters. Conversely, warm autumn SST lead to colder winters in Northern Europe and warmer winters in Southern Europe. The Iberian Peninsula, parts of Turkey, and the Baltic region are iden- tified as relevant areas that are mostly impacted by cold and warm SST. The findings suggest that monitoring autumn SST patterns in the North Atlantic, particularly the tripolar phase, SPG and STG could help in forecasting European winter temperature anomalies.

## Notebooks
1. TempChange Jupyter notebook analyzes European winter temperature changes from 1880 to 2014 relative to the 1850-1879 baseline. The notebook features a plot visualizing these temperature trends over time, offering insights into long-term climatic shifts. It is designed to provide a clear understanding of how winter temperatures in Europe have evolved, using historical data.

2. CorrAnalysis Jupyter notebook explores the correlation between autumn North Atlantic sea surface temperatures (SST) and winter European temperatures. The analysis investigates how changes in SST during the autumn months may influence or predict winter climate patterns across Europe. Through data visualization and statistical methods, the notebook provides insights into the relationship between oceanic conditions and seasonal temperature variations

3. DeepModel_1 notebook focuses on predicting warm and cold autumn North Atlantic sea surface temperatures (SST) using winter 2mT temperature data from Europe. The model leverages deep learning techniques to analyze the relationship between winter temperatures and autumn SST conditions. Additionally, the notebook implements Layer-wise Relevance Propagation (LRP) rules to identify the most relevant input areas, providing insights into which factors significantly influence the predictions.

3. DeepModel_2 notebook focuses on predicting warm and cold winter 2mT temperature from autumn North Atlantic sea surface temperatures (SST).
